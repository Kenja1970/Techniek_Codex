Controls and operations BAS trends, setpoints, overrides, simultaneous heating and cooling, and the drift that returns after every fix. Action and follow-up Prioritized actions with an owner, an expected impact, the operating limits, and a verification method. From data to verified action Energy work only pays when a finding becomes a specific operating change someone is accountable for. Collect the data Utility bills, interval data, BAS trends, schedules, equipment lists, and the operating constraints that limit change.

Useful when

Energy work becomes valuable when findings turn into specific operating changes, capital decisions, or maintenance actions.

  • Utility costs are rising and the cause is unclear.
  • Building automation data exists but is not being reviewed consistently.
  • Operators need help prioritizing which energy findings deserve action first.
  • Management needs a clear explanation of savings opportunities and operational constraints.

Opportunity scan output

Engagement process

A clear path from data review to operational action.

Collect the data

Gather utility bills, interval data, BAS trends, schedules, equipment lists, and operating constraints.

Find the patterns

Look for load drift, scheduling issues, demand peaks, simultaneous loads, and anomalies worth verifying.

Assign actions

Turn findings into specific next steps with owner, expected value, safety limits, and follow-up date.

A good first message

“We run a 90,000 sq ft manufacturing plant. Our chilled water system has tripped three times this quarter, and the vendor wants to replace the chiller — which I can’t justify to ownership without a second opinion.

We need to decide before the capital request closes on 30 September. I can send the trend logs, the vendor’s proposal, and the original mechanical drawings.”

Four sentences is plenty: what you run, what is stuck, when it matters, and what you can share. If you have less than this, send it anyway.

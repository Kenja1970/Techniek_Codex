What to prepare before contacting Techniek

The finder gives a starting recommendation. A better first conversation comes from a few clear inputs that explain the decision, constraint, and urgency.

  • Describe the asset, facility, project phase, or operating problem.
  • Identify the decision you need to make and when you need to make it.
  • List what records are available, such as drawings, logs, photos, schedules, bills, trends, or reports.
  • Flag whether any supporting records may include FCI, CUI, export-controlled data, credentials, pricing, or client-confidential details.

Good first question

"Here is the issue, here is the decision we need to make, and here is what we already know." That is enough to start a useful technical conversation.

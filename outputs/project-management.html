Where Techniek helps most

The fix is rarely more meetings. It is a clearer record and a predictable rhythm for decisions.

  • Many stakeholders, and no clear owner for the next action.
  • RFIs, submittals, or design changes are quietly driving the schedule.
  • An owner needs a plain-language view without losing the technical detail.
  • The project needs a reset before it gets more expensive to correct.

What a control view contains

  • Every open item with an owner and a date
  • Decisions made, and what they were based on
  • Risks ranked by what they threaten
  • One weekly status page for the owner

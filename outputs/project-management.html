Where Techniek helps most

The highest-value work is often not more meetings. It is a clearer project record and a better rhythm for decisions.

  • Projects with many stakeholders and unclear ownership of next actions.
  • Technical work where RFIs, submittals, or design changes are creating schedule pressure.
  • Owners who need a plain-language project view without losing technical detail.
  • Teams that need a reset before the project becomes more expensive to correct.

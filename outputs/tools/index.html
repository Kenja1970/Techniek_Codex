Practical screening tools for faster technical decisions.

Configure an engineering case, expose the governing assumptions, and produce concise evidence for the next level of review.

Available tools

Built for engineers who need a defensible starting point without hunting through disconnected inputs and spreadsheets.

Techniek OpsBoard Pro

Local-first Kanban and portfolio board for engineering operations: editable columns with WIP limits, resource utilization, PMI/PMBOK earned value (CPI/SPI/EAC), Gantt with critical path, risk register, and role-gated manager and client reports. Runs entirely in your browser.

Techniek TwinSim Studio

Local-first digital-twin and token-based discrete-event simulation studio for manufacturing, logistics, and engineering production queues. Build a line on the canvas, run replicated simulations, and review throughput, bottlenecks, and what-if scenarios with confidence ranges.

Flange Capacity Explorer

Compare standard-rated and compact flanges under pressure, tensile axial load, and bending moment. Select flange and bolt materials, review stress and seal-contact screening, and download calculation evidence.

Engineering Support

Clear technical guidance before scope, cost, or schedule moves forward.

Techniek Engineering helps owners, operators, and project teams review technical questions, organize options, and turn uncertainty into practical next steps.

Discuss a Technical Question

Best for focused reviews, planning support, and project decision points.

Typical starting point

A system, design, vendor proposal, facility issue, or project decision needs a clear technical basis before the team commits resources.

What this service covers

Focused engineering support that makes decisions easier to explain, defend, and execute.

Technical Review

Review drawings, equipment information, site conditions, vendor proposals, project assumptions, and operating constraints.

Decision Support

Compare practical options, identify tradeoffs, summarize risks, and recommend a clear next action.

Documentation

Create concise technical summaries, review notes, issue logs, and owner-facing documentation.

Useful when

Bring Techniek in when the team has enough information to ask a real question, but not enough structure to make a confident decision.

  • A project has conflicting technical opinions or unclear assumptions.
  • An owner needs a second set of eyes before approving a proposal.
  • A facility issue keeps returning and needs a more disciplined review.
  • A non-technical stakeholder needs the engineering issue explained clearly.

Common deliverables

  • Technical review memo
  • Options and tradeoff summary
  • Issue and risk register
  • Recommended next-step plan

Engagement process

A simple workflow keeps the work useful, scoped, and easy to act on.

Define the decision

Clarify what the team needs to decide, by when, and what information is available.

Review the evidence

Assess technical information, operating constraints, stakeholder needs, and project risk.

Recommend next steps

Deliver a concise summary that supports action, follow-up, or deeper engineering work.

Need a practical technical review?

Send the situation, what decision is pending, and any available supporting documents.
